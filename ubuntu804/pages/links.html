Links and other protocols

HTTP is the noisiest way to reach this machine, not the only one. Everything below is live. If one of them is not, the status page will say so before this page does.

Ways in

Gopher — port 70
gopher://dreamstation.systems — served by gophernicus. The same content, minus the desktop.
Gemini — port 1965
gemini://dreamstation.systems — served by molly-brown, over TLS, as Gemini insists.
Spartan — port 300
spartan://dreamstation.systems — a hand‐rolled server that serves the Gemini capsule over Spartan, which is Gemini with the TLS taken out and the ceremony reduced further.
Finger — port 79
finger @dreamstation.systems — RFC 1288, hand-rolled. Try finger openget@dreamstation.systems too.
DICT — port 2628
dict -h dreamstation.systems word — dictd, with the usual dictionaries.
QOTD — port 17
nc dreamstation.systems 17 — a quote, RFC 865, hand‐rolled.
FTP — port 21
ftp://dreamstation.systems — vsftpd, anonymous, read‐only. An archive, not a drop box.
NTP and NTS — ports 123 and 4460
The actual job. This machine is in pool.ntp.org, and serves NTS for clients that would like their time authenticated.
The small services — ports 13, 7, 9, 19
daytime (RFC 867), echo (RFC 862), discard (RFC 863) and chargen (RFC 864). All hand‐rolled. nc dreamstation.systems 19 will talk until you stop it.
SMTP, IMAP, submission — ports 25, 143, 465, 587, 993
Postfix, Dovecot and OpenDKIM, serving exactly one mailbox: robin@dreamstation.systems.
