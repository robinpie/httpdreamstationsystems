These are my typographic guidelines for smart text on the W3 site on dreamstation.systems. This is a note for me, it is not served on the website (which is why it is not ASCII‐only).

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ The character set                                                           │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Use in HTML, including e.g. script‐generated HTML:

Use U+2019 ’, not '.
Use U+201C/U+201D “/”, not ".
Don't use - U+002D HYPHEN-MINUS in English prose. Depending on the situation, use ‐ U+2010 HYPHEN (compounds), — U+2014 EM DASH, ‒ U+2012 FIGURE DASH (for phone numbers), and – U+2013 EN DASH for ranges. Use - U+002D HYPHEN-MINUS where it appears in literal proper names, technical terms, or where preserving verbatim uses of the text: e.g. dinky-git, NTS-KE, GPT-2, Attribution-NonCommercial.
Use   U+00A0 NO-BREAK SPACE to bind a number to its unit in flowing text. For holding a long run together, prefer CSS.
Use … U+2026 HORIZONTAL ELLIPSIS, not ....
Use 2190–21FF arrows like →, not ASCII approximations.

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Em dashes                                                                   │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

A parenthetical dash should usually be thin‐space + em dash + thin‐space, unless there is a reason to do otherwise.

    U+2009 U+2014 U+2009

    ...feels exotic, or even dangerous — see the very common ironic fear...

One example of an exception is em dashes as a placeholder:

    professional/index.html   empty skills-table cell:  <td>—</td>
    cgi/status.cgi:1064       esc($c->{leap} // '—')
    tlltdIslandDrafter        X: &mdash; &nbsp; Y: &mdash;

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Where the rules do not apply                                                │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Plaintext served as text/plain, for example /llms.txt  /humans.txt  /robots.txt  /.well-known/security.txt. Do not put non‐ASCII in the middle of a sentence to avoid mojibake due to locale-dependent fallback encodings. Non-essential ASCII that's not in the middle of a sentence is an accepted risk, e.g. in /humans.txt. Rules DO apply to this document because it is not served.

Code.

Do not retypeset someone else's words, including machine‐generated text.

Do not change rootdomain/old/ and rootdomain/oldsite/.

Documents typically read as part of a repository, such as personal/README.md, oldsite/README.md and personal/ath/!~ATH spec.md.

ASCII art.
