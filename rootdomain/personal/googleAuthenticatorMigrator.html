Google Authenticator → andOTP Converter

You can use this to move your Google Authenticator accounts to other tools that support andOTP format, such as Keysmith.

Paste your otpauth-migration:// URI below and click Convert.

To get your otpauth-migration:// URI, open Google Authenticator → hamburger menu → Transfer codes → Export codes → select the codes you want to export → run the QR code through a standard scanning tool.


 

Runs entirely in your browser; no data is transmitted over the internet. But don’t take my word for it, check the code yourself.
